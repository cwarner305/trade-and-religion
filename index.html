Key Words
Merchant
A person who buys and sells goods.
Caravanserai
A stopping place where caravans could find shelter, water, food, storage, and safety.
Credit
Receiving money or goods now with a promise to pay later.
Diffusion
The spread of an idea, belief, technology, or cultural trait from one place to another.
Adaptation
Changing an idea or practice so it fits a new place or culture.
Transculturation
Long-term cultural interaction that creates new or blended cultural forms.
Diaspora
A community of people living away from their ancestral homeland while keeping connections to a shared identity or tradition.
Institution
An organized part of society, such as a monastery, mosque, church, school, government office, or market.

Essay Question: How can economic connections lead to religious and cultural change?
Sentence frame:
Trade created economic opportunity because ____________________. This increased connection between ____________________ and ____________________. As merchants, travelers, migrants, and religious communities moved, ____________________ interacted with ____________________. This led to ____________________, showing that economic connection can cause cultural change.
Possible claim: Long-distance trade created economic opportunities that brought more people into contact. As merchants and travelers moved along trade routes, beliefs entered new cultural settings where they could spread, coexist, and adapt.
